# Lesson Plan — Graphing Rational Functions

**Course:** Precalculus (11th grade)
**Duration:** 50 minutes
**Unit:** Rational Functions, Day 3 of 6

## Objective

Students will understand rational functions and be able to graph them.

## Standards

F-IF.7d — Graph rational functions, identifying zeros and asymptotes.

## Materials

- Notes packet (pages 12–14)
- Graphing calculators
- Homework worksheet 6.3

## Warm-Up (5 min)

Put these on the board. Students work silently.

1. Factor: x² − 9
2. Factor: x² + 5x + 6
3. State the domain: f(x) = 1/x

Go over answers.

## Direct Instruction (20 min)

Walk through the notes packet on the document camera.

Define vertical asymptote, horizontal asymptote, and hole. Students copy the
definitions into the packet.

Give them the rules for horizontal asymptotes:

- If degree of numerator < degree of denominator, HA is y = 0
- If the degrees are equal, HA is the ratio of leading coefficients
- If degree of numerator > degree of denominator, there is no HA

Work Example 1 on the board: f(x) = (x + 2)/(x − 3). Show the steps — find the
vertical asymptote, find the horizontal asymptote, plot a few points, sketch.

Work Example 2 the same way: g(x) = (x² − 4)/(x² − x − 6). Point out the hole
at x = −2.

Ask if there are any questions.

## Guided Practice (15 min)

Students do problems 1–6 in the packet at their seats. Walk around and help
anyone who is stuck.

If students are struggling, give them the step-by-step reference sheet with the
procedure written out so they can follow along.

For students who finish early, they can start the homework.

## Closure (5 min)

Review the answers to 1–6. Remind students of the three horizontal asymptote
rules.

## Homework

Worksheet 6.3, problems 1–20 (odds).

## Differentiation

- Struggling students: give the step-by-step reference sheet, and allow them to
  do only the even problems.
- Advanced students: extra credit problem on the back of the worksheet.

## Assessment

Check homework completion tomorrow. Quiz on Friday.
